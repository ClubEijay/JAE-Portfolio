The Competition

LAMBO 2026 — themed "Practical AI: Pagtubo sa Negosyo" — is Cebu's premier AI Business Summit, held on June 27, 2026 at the Waterfront Cebu City Hotel & Casino. Co-presented by JCI Cebu, EXL, and the Asian Institute of Management, the summit gathered business leaders and the government sector around one question: how can artificial intelligence create real, practical growth for businesses? The day featured keynotes led by AI scientist Dr. Christopher Monterola, live AI demonstrations, executive panels — and, as its centerpiece, the finals of the AI-powered Business Case Competition.


The road to the finals ran through JCI Cebu's AI Business Case Training Series — three intensive sessions where industry experts in AI readiness, business intelligence, and AI transformation coached all 18 competing university teams on applying AI to real-world business challenges. The guiding principle: AI should sharpen critical thinking, not replace it.


Competing as the Visayas Visionaries, we put that principle to work — pairing grounded business analysis with practical AI application to build a case the judges could see working in the real world. When the results were read on summit night, our two-person team stood at the top of the field: 1st Place, Champions of the LAMBO 2026 Business Case Competition, with a ₱25,000 grand prize.


More than the check and the crystal, the win validated the way Team VERPTO works: small team, sharp thinking, and technology applied where it actually moves the needle — the same approach we bring to every project we ship.
