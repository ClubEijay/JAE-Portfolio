The Competition

The Innovation Challenge 2026, presented by the Philippine Society of Information Technology Educators (PSITE) – Central Visayas Region and sponsored by RAFI Microfinance, challenged student teams across the region under one theme: "AI Innovation for Financial Inclusion." Teams could compete across three battlegrounds — financial services innovation, productivity tools for microfinance, and financial literacy & customer experience — with one clear rule of engagement: bring a working prototype, not just a deck.


From the submission round, only the Top 6 teams across Cebu's universities earned a spot in the finals, held live on April 30, 2026 at the UC New Cebu Coliseum as part of the Cebu ICT Student Congress 2026 — pitching their solutions on stage before judges, sponsors, and a coliseum full of IT students.


Our Winning Pitch — RUFUS

Representing the University of Cebu – Banilad as UCB-VERPTO, our three-person team — Jashmine Verdida, Eijay Pepito, and Lord Christian Beligaño — pitched RUFUS: an AI-powered assistant built for RAFI Microfinance, designed to serve both sides of the lending desk. For loan officers, RUFUS streamlines the daily grind of microfinance operations; for borrowers, it makes financial services easier to reach and understand — putting real financial inclusion behind the buzzword.


A live demo, a sharp pitch, and a problem worth solving carried UCB-VERPTO past five strong finalists to the title of Champion, PSITE Innovation Challenge 2026 — VERPTO's second championship of the year. And RUFUS? It's getting a spot in our Projects gallery soon. Watch this space.
